## What I work on

My interests converge around a practical question: **how do we make systems sense, decide, and act reliably?**

- **Robotics & embedded systems:** physical prototypes involving mechanisms, motors, sensors, and microcontrollers.
- **AI/ML:** neural-network and machine-learning experiments, including handwritten-digit classification.
- **Software systems:** Python and Swift automation, web applications, and interfaces designed around real workflows.

I am interested in moving from working demonstrations toward better engineering practice: explicit hypotheses, repeatable tests, documented limitations, and measured results.

## Technical direction

I am particularly interested in robotics, mechanisms and grasping, embedded sensing, computer vision, and the interface between physical systems and intelligent software. I am building my foundations and want to learn how to investigate engineering questions with sound experiments.

For research conversations, I value specific technical feedback and bounded problems I can investigate independently. I do not assume that a prototype alone constitutes research; the next step is to make the reasoning, testing, and results reproducible.

## Working style

**Question → Design → Build → Test → Record → Iterate**

I prefer learning by making things, then improving them through debugging and evidence. Where results are not yet measured or documented, I will say so.
